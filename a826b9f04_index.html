Before you book

Questions worth asking.

Is this for me if I'm just starting out?

It's built for owners who already have something to sell and a few clients behind them. If you're pre revenue, start with a single strategy call rather than monthly coaching.

What actually happens on the call?

You tell me the one thing that's stuck. We look at how you get clients now, find where it's breaking, and I tell you the next steps I would take if it were my agency.

Can I start with one call and decide later?

Yes. Start with one call. If ongoing coaching makes sense after we talk, I'll say so. If it doesn't, I'll say that too.

Do you only work with design agencies?

No. I've run a website design agency, but the client acquisition system is the same whether you sell design, ads, SEO, or video.

How do I know it'll work for my agency?

You don't, and I won't pretend otherwise. What I can promise is a straight read on what isn't working and the specific next steps I'd take in your position.
